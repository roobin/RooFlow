# RooFlow notebook

This folder is a RooFlow notebook; the rules come from the rooflow plugin (they are added when a session starts here).
Settings are in `rooflow.json`. Commands are listed in `commands.md`.

## Own rules

Add your own rules here: who you are, who your colleagues are (first names), how you want answers, where things may be stored.
The plugin never writes in this file after it was created.
