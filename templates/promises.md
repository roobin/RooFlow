# Promises

Promise: `- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD (deadline) · to WHOM · WHAT · source`. Checked off with `[x]`, never deleted.

Waiting (you wait for someone else): `- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD (since) · waiting for WHOM · WHAT · source`.
Such lines show under WAITING in the overview, not under PROMISED. Use first names only.
